Deeper is a puzzle-platformer about a deep-sea diver uncovering ancient ruins. It started as a Ludum Dare 48 entry built solo in 48 hours, with the theme "Deeper and Deeper."

The core mechanic is navigating cramped underwater corridors with limited oxygen, finding paths between air pockets. Eight levels were shipped for the jam.

Post-jam I've been slowly expanding the level count and tightening the art. No release date set.

**Tools:** Godot 3.3, Aseprite, BFXR
