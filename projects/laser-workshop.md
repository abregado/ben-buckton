An ongoing series of builds using a CO2 laser cutter. Started with learning the basics — kerf compensation, material settings, safe ventilation — and has grown into a proper workshop practice.

Projects have included enclosures, custom game components, decorative panels, and gifts. The machine is a mid-range Chinese CO2 unit running LightBurn.

**Machine:** 60W CO2 laser cutter  
**Software:** LightBurn
